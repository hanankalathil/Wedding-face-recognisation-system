Privacy Policy

Your privacy and data security are our top priority.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

At Techora Memories, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered wedding photo-sharing platform.

2. How We Use Selfies and Facial Data

We use facial recognition technology to securely match you with wedding photos.

  • Matching Only: Selfies uploaded to our platform are strictly used for the purpose of matching your face against the wedding gallery.
  • No Permanent Storage: Selfies and generated biometric data are processed ephemerally. We do not store your selfies or facial data permanently after the matching process is complete.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your biometric data is never sold, traded, or shared with third parties.

3. Data Processing and Security

All photos are securely processed using industry-standard encryption. Our servers employ strict security protocols to prevent unauthorized access.

4. Guest Control

Guests have full control over their data. You can request the removal of any matched photos from your device, and you may choose to opt-out of facial recognition entirely by contacting the event hosts or our support team.
